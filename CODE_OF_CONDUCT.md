# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MyoSim contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to foster a respectful, inclusive, evidence-led research-software community. We welcome participation regardless of background, identity, experience level, or discipline.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should communicate constructively, review claims against evidence, acknowledge limitations, protect personal and sensitive information, respect licenses and attribution, and accept good-faith technical feedback. Research discussion must not present MyoSim as a clinically validated device or use the project to provide patient-specific advice.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, plagiarism, knowingly false technical or research claims, publication of private data, and sharing credentials or sensitive biosignal/person data are unacceptable. Do not use public project channels to disclose a suspected security vulnerability; follow `SECURITY.md` instead.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner or host moderation channel. Maintainers may clarify expectations, request correction, remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community and the integrity of the project.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and other project-sponsored spaces. It is a community standard, not a substitute for local law, institutional ethics review, or medical/regulatory governance.
